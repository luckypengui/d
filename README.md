# Neon Dash

You are a blue square and must dodge the red balls falling from above.

- **PC:** arrow keys or A/D, or just move the mouse. Space/P pauses, Space/Enter starts.
- **Mobile:** tap/hold on the left or right half of the screen.
- The more points you have, the faster it gets. Your best score is saved in the browser.
- The game pauses automatically when you switch tabs.
